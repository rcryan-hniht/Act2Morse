OVERVIEW

Assistive Technology for Impaired Individuals

Act2Morse bridges the communication gap for deaf-mute, non-verbal, and ALS/motor-impaired individuals by converting micro-gestures and eye blinks into live Morse code and synthesized speech.

MODE 1

Fin2Morse (Tactile Tap)

Designed for individuals with subtle finger or hand mobility.

  • Screen Tap or Spacebar: Press & hold anywhere on screen or use spacebar.
  • Dot (•): Short hold under 380ms.
  • Dash (—): Hold longer than 380ms.
  • Hotkeys: Press D for Dot, F for Dash, Space to finalize letter.
MODE 2

Blink2Morse (Eye Tracking)

Hands-free ocular mode for individuals with limited limb movement.

  • Start Camera: Click Start Camera to activate browser/Python MediaPipe tracking.
  • Natural Blinks: Brief eye flutters under 80ms are filtered out as noise.
  • Conscious Blink: 80–600ms produces a Dot; >600ms produces a Dash.
  • Auto-decode: Pausing 1.2s finalizes the letter; 2.5s adds a word space.
